# Welcome to your brain

This folder is HAL's window into your second brain. Anything you drop in here —
Markdown notes, journals, project plans, logs — HAL can search, read, and (when
you ask) write to.

A few ideas to get started:

- **Daily log** — tell HAL "log that I decided X today" and it appends to a dated note.
- **Projects** — keep a note per project; ask HAL "what's the status of the exam pass?"
- **Decisions** — record why you chose something so future-you (and HAL) remember.

Point HAL at your real notes with `--brain "path/to/your/2nd Brain"` or by setting
the `HAL_BRAIN_DIR` environment variable. This starter note is safe to delete.
